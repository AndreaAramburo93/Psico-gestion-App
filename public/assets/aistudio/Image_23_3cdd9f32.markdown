## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a serene, empathetic, and rigorously professional clinical environment tailored for psychological practice management and tele-mental health. It consciously departs from cold clinical portals and cluttered administrative dashboards, grounding itself in therapeutic tranquility, psychological safety, and cognitive ease.

The aesthetic philosophy draws on organic minimalism enriched with soft tactile warmth:
- **Atmospheric Calm:** Generous negative space, whisper-soft sage undertones, and gentle contrast alleviate anxiety for both clinicians and seeking patients.
- **Human Connection:** Subdued botanical tones combined with soft peach accents introduce warmth, empathy, and approachable vulnerability without sacrificing medical authority.
- **Cognitive Clarity:** Structured data presentation, unambiguous appointment lifecycles, and distraction-free documentation canvases support seamless consultations and recordkeeping.

## Colors

The color palette is calibrated to evoke restfulness, emotional stabilization, and clarity. The foundation pairs soft botanical greens with stabilizing lavender-slate blue and empathetic terracotta.

### Foundation & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAF9`):** A soft, luminous sage-porcelain background that sets a gentle visual atmosphere across all pages.
- **Card & Elevated Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp, pure white container surface establishing clear visual focus for active clinical cards and modal windows.
- **Whisper Sage Container (`#EAF1EE`):** Low-contrast surface tint reserved for table headers, inactive panel groupings, and calendar column divisions.
- **Primary Ink (`#1F2E2B`):** Deep botanical slate delivering WCAG AAA contrast while eliminating the harshness of pure black.
- **Secondary Ink (`#4E615D`):** Muted sage-charcoal for subtitles, timestamps, field hints, and non-primary metadata.
- **Hairline Border (`#E2ECE7`):** Gentle structural separator used for container framing, session cards, and calendar grid matrices.

### Interactive & Accents
- **Primary Accent (`#3D6B5E`):** Deep muted sage green indicating primary progression, session confirmations, and primary action triggers.
- **Secondary Accent (`#5B6B8C`):** Calming lavender-slate blue for contextual filters, schedule view switchers, and clinical histories.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#D9826C`):** Empathetic warm peach applied to payment triggers, emergency contacts, and sensitive wellness reminders.

### Appointment Lifecycle Tokens
- **Confirmada (Confirmed):** Forest Green (`#2E7D32`) on Soft Spring Mint (`#E8F5E9`).
- **Pendiente (Pending):** Amber Ochre (`#B26A00`) on Warm Sunny Cream (`#FFF8E1`).
- **Completada (Completed):** Quiet Slate (`#455A64`) on Muted Cloud Gray (`#ECEFF1`).
- **Cancelada (Cancelled):** Therapeutic Crimson (`#C62828`) on Soft Blush Rose (`#FFEBEE`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the warm geometric presence of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural headers and action badges with the neutral clarity of **Inter** for clinical records, forms, and analytical information.

- **Headings & Wayfinding:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides an organic, approachable voice that reduces institutional sterility. Generous negative kerning is applied to large sizes for crisp visual density.
- **Reading & Clinical Documentation:** Inter is set with an expanded 1.5x - 1.6x line-height ratio on body copy to ensure long clinical histories, case formulations, and therapeutic notes remain strain-free during prolonged review sessions.
- **Caps & Micro-Tokens:** Micro-labels and appointment state indicators employ open letter spacing (+0.04em) to maintain total clarity across varied display pixel densities.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is constructed on a flexible 12-column grid system designed around a 4px/8px base spatial increment. Content frames emphasize open breathing space to minimize visual congestion during critical workflows.

### Grid Architecture & Widths
- **Clinical Dashboard Max Width:** 1280px for patient records, directory exploration, and profile configuration.
- **Calendar & Timetable Matrix:** 1440px expanded canvas allowing multi-therapist and weekly schedule overviews.
- **Grid Configuration:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns with 24px (1.5rem) gutters and 48px (3rem) page margins.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 6 columns with 20px (1.25rem) gutters and 24px (1.5rem) page margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns or single stacked column with 16px (1rem) gutters and 16px (1rem) canvas margins.

### Spatial Rhythm
- Module separations span `space-xl` (32px) to 48px to cleanly partition distinct medical sections.
- Form controls adhere to an explicit 20px - 24px vertical separation to eliminate accidental inputs during psychiatric record keeping.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through gentle atmospheric layering and ambient tint diffusion, avoiding heavy, dark drop shadows.

- **Base Layering:** Elevated cards and panels float on the `#F8FAF9` porcelain background using a pure `#FFFFFF` fill bounded by an ultra-fine border (`1px solid #E2ECE7`).
- **Ambient Shadow Depth:** Surface elevation uses soft, low-contrast shadows tinted with dark botanical ink:
  - *Default Card Elevation:* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(31, 46, 43, 0.05)`
  - *Interactive / Floating State:* `0 10px 28px -4px rgba(31, 46, 43, 0.09)` with a -2px vertical lift.
- **Modal & Backdrop Blur:** Flyout drawers, patient booking modals, and sticky navigation bars implement an atmospheric glass veil using `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` over `rgba(248, 250, 249, 0.85)` with a subtle hairline base border.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates soft, rounded organic geometry to communicate human-centered approachability and comfort.

- **Component Corners:** Standard inputs, buttons, and minor cards utilize a 0.75rem (12px) radius.
- **Structural Cards:** Clinical consultation modules, therapist profile containers, and major viewports employ a 1rem to 1.25rem (16px - 20px) radius.
- **Status Pills & Toggles:** Appointment lifecycle indicators and modality tags use fully pill-shaped geometry (`rounded-full`, 9999px) to convey discrete self-contained states.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Styled in deep sage green (`#3D6B5E`) with white text, minimum height of 48px, rounded to 12px. Includes a 3px soft sage focus halo (`#E4EFEA`) on active states.
- **Secondary Action:** Styled in soft lavender container (`#E8EDF5`) with deep lavender-slate text (`#243350`), providing an accessible alternative for profile inspection and agenda filtering.
- **Tertiary Empathy Action:** Styled in muted terracotta peach (`#D9826C`) with white text, reserved for empathetic checkout triggers and emergency links.
- **Ghost / Outline Button:** Hairline border (`#CBD8D2`) over transparent fill with charcoal text (`#4E615D`), hovering to `#EAF1EE`.

### Appointment Status Chips
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), 28px height, 12px horizontal padding, with uppercase 11px/12px bold typography accompanied by a semantic 6px status icon or dot indicator:
  - **Confirmada:** `#2E7D32` text/icon over `#E8F5E9` background.
  - **Pendiente:** `#B26A00` text/icon over `#FFF8E1` background.
  - **Completada:** `#455A64` text/icon over `#ECEFF1` background.
  - **Cancelada:** `#C62828` text/icon over `#FFEBEE` background.

### Input Fields & Forms
- Surfaces use white or porcelain fills framed by a 1.5px border in `#CBD8D2` and a 12px corner radius.
- Focus displays a crisp border color shift to `#3D6B5E` alongside an ambient halo ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(61, 107, 94, 0.15)`).
- Dedicated auto-expanding clinical text areas provide distraction-free note taking with ruled left indicators and auto-save indicators.

### Cards & Patient Summaries
- Pure white container base with a 1px `#E2ECE7` border, 24px internal padding, and ambient shadow.
- Patient metadata displays structured left-aligned rows, accompanied by modality badges ("Virtual / Meet", "Presencial - Consultorio 302") and discrete price indicators.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Built with a 20x20px tactile hit box. Checked states activate the `#3D6B5E` sage fill with a crisp white mark, utilizing a 6px corner radius for checkboxes and a circular format for radio controls.

### Weekly Schedule Matrix
- Time-grid slots with subtle `#E2ECE7` division borders. Session blocks contain a solid 4px colored left edge matching the appointment status, displaying the scheduled hour, patient name, and quick contextual actions.
